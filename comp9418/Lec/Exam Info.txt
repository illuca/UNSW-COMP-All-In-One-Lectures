Information About The Final Exam
This is important information about the final exam. Please post all your questions (technical or otherwise) about the exam here.

Duration
2 hours.

What To Bring
Candidates may bring UNSW approved calculators to the examination. No other materials or notes may be brought by candidates. 
Exam Structure
The exam will contain exactly 4 questions. Each question is worth 25 marks for a total of 25x4=100 marks.

Examinable Material 
All the material studied during the course (lecture slides, compulsory reading, tutorials, assessment) is examinable. As the exam is paper-based, there will not be coding exercises. Only the following topics are exceptions to this rule:

Sampling [Week 6]: There will not be a question on the exam regarding this topic.
Continuous latent variables [Week 7]: There will not be a question on the exam regarding this topic.
Types of Questions
You can expect to be asked conceptual questions, mathematical questions (i.e. mathematical derivations) and short calculations (so you may need a calculator for this). You do not need to memorise Gaussian identities such as the expression for the conditional distribution given a joint Gaussian or the integral of the product of two Gaussians. You will be given these if there are questions that require them. However, well-known expressions that you should be familiar with (e.g. the expression for a joint Gaussian distribution) will not be given.
