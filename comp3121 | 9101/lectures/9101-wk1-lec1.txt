            h offers a job to its favourite doctor that hasn't got any offer and ignore any other doctor to it.

## Prove that it will terminate:
For each round, one hospital offers 1 job to 1 doctor, then each round has n offers and each round makes at least 1 doctor get a job.

Assume that there is a hospital vacant.
Because there is every hospital will finally offer job to every doctor. Then every doctor will get a job because a doctor will aceept any job if he gets no offer.
Then n doctor gets job, so there is no hospital vacant.

## Prove that every pair is stable.
Assume there is two pairs, (h,d) and (h',d')
h prefers d' to d
d' prefers h to h'
