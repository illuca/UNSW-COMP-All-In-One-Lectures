Information on the COMP9334 final exam

Mode of attendence

  • Domestic students and onshore international students must attend in-person for their on-campus invigilated exam. In-person attendance at the Sydney Campus for your final exam is a mandatory requirement for this course.
  • Offshore international students, who are unable to travel to Australia for the Exam Period, will be invigilated remotely using Zoom.

Inspera and exam rules

All students will use the Inspera platform for their exam. However, the requirements are slightly different for on-campus and remotely invigilated exams; this will be explained below.

General information on how UNSW uses the Inspera platform is available at this Inspera@UNSW page . The Inspera@UNSW page also has links to pages with information on on-campus and remotely invigilated exams. We ask you to carefully go through the Inspera@UNSW page, as well as the linked pages available from there. We will highlight some information below, but you are responsbile to go through all the contents on Inspera@UNSW page, as well as its linked pages, yourselves.


On-campus invigilated exam Remotely invigilated exam
Inspera In the exam, you will use the Safe Exam Browser (SEB) to access Inspera. You will need to bring your own device and you should install SEB on your device before coming to the exam.

On the On-Campus Invigilated Final & Supplementary Exams page, you will find information on:
  • Hardware and software requirements for installing the SEB.
  • Note that SEB only works on Windows or Mac OS. This means you cannot use devices that use other operating systems, such as iOS, Android etc. If you do not have a suitable device, the page tells you how you can borrow a laptop from the university.
  • There are instructions to download and test SEB.
You do not need to use the Safe Exam Browser. You will access Inspera using a web browser.

On the Off-Campus Final & Supplementary Exams page, you will find information on:
  • System and browser requirements for using Inspera.
  • How to access Inspera.
  • Phone number and email that you can use on the day of the exam in case you have questions to ask. The university does not allow students to contact the teaching staff directly during the exam.
  • What you should do in case of network issues.
Zoom Not applicable Offshore students are required to join a Zoom meeting. They must remain in this Zoom meeting, keep their camera, speakers and screensharing on for the entirety of their exam.
What you can take into the exam The page What You Can Take Into Exams has lists on what you must bring, what you can bring, what you cannot bring. Check the lists carefully and do not get caught by surprise. Note that:
  • You must bring your student ID.
  • You will need your smartphone for multi-factor authentication.
  • You should bring a university approved calculator.
  • For COMP9334, you are allowed to bring hard copy of the course materials.
Softcopy of course materials

We will be providing you with softcopy of course materials via Inspera. In your Inspera exam, there is a link (located at the bottom-left hand corner) called "Course Materials" and that link points to this page which contains soft copy of the course materials. You will be able to see the "Course Materials" link in the COMP9334 sample exam.

Any other methods of viewing soft copies of course materials are prohibited. In particular, you will not be allowed to view your personal soft copy of the course materials.
